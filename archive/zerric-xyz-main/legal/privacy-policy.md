# Privacy Policy — zerric.xyz

**Effective Date:** 2026-08-24
**Applies to:** zerric.xyz and its subdomains, including snowsnakes.zerric.xyz (collectively, "the Site").

---

## 1. WHO WE ARE
The Site is operated by Zerric Dotcom (the "Owner"). For privacy questions, contact
**ez@zerric.xyz** or **zdotconnect@gmail.com**.

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
We collect information you choose to give us:

- **Account registration (SnowSnakes):** username, password (stored hashed), and — if you provide it —
  an email address.
- **User-generated content:** dad jokes, game uploads, comics, doodles, characters, comments, likes,
  and shares you post.
- **Technical data:** browser type, device, pages visited, and basic usage data via standard web logs
  and your browser's local storage (e.g., saved high scores, theme preferences).
- **Lead form (where present):** name and email if you submit a form (e.g., the business checklist).

We do **not** knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## 3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
- To operate the Site: display your content, power accounts, likes/comments, and games.
- **To manage our contact/CRM relationship:** if you register with an email, that email may be added
  to a contact list / CRM (HubSpot) so the Owner can respond to inquiries and — only with your consent
  or where lawful — send updates about the Site. You can ask to be removed at any time.
- To improve the Site: understand what content and games people enjoy.
- To keep the Site safe: prevent abuse, spam, and prohibited content.

## 4. WHAT WE DO NOT DO
- We do **not sell** your personal information.
- We do **not** share your email with third parties for their own marketing.
- We do **not** require an email to use most features — you can browse and play without registering.

## 5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
The Site runs on hosting and tools that may process limited data on our behalf:

- **Hosting:** Hostinger (site hosting, email).
- **CRM / contact management:** HubSpot (if you register with an email, contact info may be synced there).
- **Media/other tools:** services used to serve images, games, or analytics (e.g., Cloudinary, Netlify).
- **Email:** ez@zerric.xyz (Hostinger mail) for correspondence.

These providers have their own privacy policies. We use them only to run the Site.

## 6. COOKIES & LOCAL STORAGE
The Site uses browser local storage for features like high scores, theme choices, and simple
preferences. We do not use invasive third-party ad trackers. You can clear local storage in your
browser settings at any time.

## 7. YOUR RIGHTS & CHOICES
You may:
- **Access / correct / delete** your account or content: email ez@zerric.xyz and we'll help.
- **Opt out** of any update emails at any time (just ask, or use the unsubscribe link if provided).
- **Request removal** of content you posted.

We will honor reasonable requests promptly. Where required by law (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), we will comply
with applicable rights (access, deletion, portability, do-not-sell) and not discriminate against you
for exercising them.

## 8. DATA SECURITY
We use reasonable safeguards (hashed passwords, HTTPS) to protect your data. No method is 100%
secure, and you share content at your own discretion.

## 9. RETENTION
We keep account/content data while your account is active or as needed to operate the Site, and as
required by law. Deleted content is removed from public view promptly.

## 10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
We may update this policy. The "Effective Date" at the top reflects the latest version. Material
changes will be noted on the Site.

## 11. CONTACT
Questions or requests: **ez@zerric.xyz** (or zdotconnect@gmail.com). Please include "Privacy" in the
subject line.
