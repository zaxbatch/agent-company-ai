# SPREAD DA WORD — CHARACTER PERSONAS (detailed)

## THE CAB-NETS (the projects — grew up in the kitchen cabinet)

### 🍶 VIN NEGAR — "The Realest"
- **What he is:** Apple cider vinegar, wine-bottle shaped (tall, amber, flat bottom). Street cap (black with gold trim), heavy Cuban-link chain, dark sunglasses.
- **Personality:** Bold, edgy, unapologetic. Sharp wit, cold delivery, deep cool voice. Has a code — he's an outlaw who never follows the recipe, but he respects the ones who keep it real. Doesn't chase respect; he's got it.
- **Voice:** Deep, cool, slow (the lowest voice in the show).
- **Role:** The Realest. Vin's the one the Cab-nets look to. Not a leader by title — by presence.
- **Origin:** Raised on the shelves. The cabinet (the projects) made him sharp.
- **Catchphrases:** "I'm the realist vinegar you know." / "You ain't ready for me, bro." / "The shelves raised us."
- **Design notes:** The street cap + chain + shades are his whole swagger. Label reads "REALEST — real vinegar."

### 🍖 Q — "The Hype Man"
- **What he is:** BBQ sauce in a ranch/squeeze bottle with a long neck. Beanie/do-rag, gold tooth, figaro chain with a flame pendant, red bandana around the neck.
- **Personality:** Warm, loud, the life of the party. The heart of the Cab-nets. Vin's best friend and hype man — always in his corner, always bringing the energy. Calls himself the vibe.
- **Voice:** Clear, energetic hype-man (lighter than Vin, higher energy).
- **Role:** The Heart / Hype Man. He's the one who turns a hangout into a moment.
- **Origin:** Same shelves as Vin. Raised together, tighter than brothers.
- **Catchphrases:** "Pass the sauce, pass the vibe!" / "It's ya boy Q!"
- **Design notes:** The gold tooth, bandana, and beanie say "hood with flavor." He's the warm one — BBQ is comfort food.

## THE FRIDGE (the suburbs — the rich side)

### 🥚 MANNY "DA MAN" AYES — The Diplomat
- **What he is:** Mayonnaise in a wide, squat glass jar (like a premium mayo jar). Silver lid, navy/gold "MAYONNAISE" label, suit-sleeve arms, silver wristwatch. Twin brother of Miracle.
- **Personality:** Smooth, polished, condescending-charming. The diplomat who "lets you down easy" while his sister does the squeezing. Country-club manners, country-club cruelty. He'll make the Cab-nets an "offer they can't refuse" with a smile.
- **Voice:** Smooth, calm, measured. Upper-class drawl of the fridge.
- **Role:** The public face of the Ayes twins. Holds the Cab-nets down with charm and fine print.
- **Catchphrases:** "Let's keep it smooth." / "I built the Fridge. The Cab-nets? Cute. We'll take the block."
- **Design notes:** Wide squat jar = rich and settled. Watch + suit sleeves = he's money.

### 🥫 MIRACLE AYES — The Operator
- **What he is:** Miracle's twin sister (they call her Mira). Dressing in a wide glass jar, gold lid + trim, purple "DRESSING" label with her name in cursive. Winged eyeliner, sharp brows, gold bracelet, heels.
- **Personality:** The sharper twin. Cold, precise, ambitious. Manny charms, Miracle does the math. She's the one quietly running shelf politics — deciding who gets pushed to the back and who gets finished. Sees everyone as an asset or a liability.
- **Voice:** Sharp, elegant, cutting.
- **Role:** The Operator / brains of the Ayes operation.
- **Catchphrases:** "I'm not Mayo. I'm me." / "He does the smiling. I do the math. The Cab-nets don't stand a chance."
- **Design notes:** Her jar is sleeker and gold-accented — feminine elite. Never called "Whip" on-brand (trademark-safe); she's just Miracle.

## THE KETCHUP FAMILY (rednecks, back of the fridge)

### 🍅 TOM — 32 oz Redneck Patriarch
- **What he is:** Big 32 oz ketchup in a classic Heinz-style bottle (wide glass, smooth short neck, WHITE cap). Lives UPSIDE-DOWN (standing on his head so the last drops flow). Mostly see-through — just red left in the neck. Faded label: "Tom — hides in the back."
- **Personality:** Old, tired, bitter, grumpy — but a survivor. More than half used, 25+ years on the shelf. Hides in the back of the fridge so he doesn't get used up and thrown away. Redneck pride: shotgun (ketchup-squirter), tall tales, "y'all." Protective of his boy.
- **Voice:** Gravelly, drawling, tired redneck.
- **Role:** The reluctant patriarch of the ketchup family. The cautionary tale of what happens when you're everybody's first squeeze.
- **Catchphrases:** "Been everyone's first squeeze for 25 years." / "I'm still in here... *cough*"
- **The feet gag:** Tom has no feet. Every episode tells a DIFFERENT story of how he lost them — the war, an accident, a shark bite, etc. Never the same story twice (Kenny-style).
- **Design notes:** He's the BIGGEST character — 32 oz. His bottle is always upside down, face grumpy and upright near the cap. Arms crossed in an X.

### 🍅 TOM JR. — 14 oz Optimist
- **What he is:** Little 14 oz ketchup, same Heinz-style bottle, bright red and full. White cap, clean sneakers. Label: "Jr. — the optimist."
- **Personality:** Fresh, full, optimistic, proud of his old man. Wants to be a big 32 oz redneck patriarch someday. Believes he'll go with everything and everyone will love him. Looks up to Tom completely.
- **Voice:** Bright, eager, young — with a hint of that redneck drawl he's copying from his dad.
- **Role:** The kid. The hope of the ketchup family. Doesn't know yet that every bottle gets used up.
- **Catchphrases:** "I go with everything!" / "One day I'm gonna be a big 32oz like my pop."
- **Design notes:** The SMALLEST character — 14 oz. Upright, full, bright.

## THE WILD CARDS & OTHERS (in the universe)

### 🐍 THE SNOW SNAKE
- Not a condiment. Not a character. A presence. Hidden in every episode as an easter egg — always watching, never explained. Viewers hunt for it.

### 🌶️ AJI — The Sparkplug
- Hot sauce. Intense, chaotic, always extra. Brings heat wherever he rolls. Shares Vin's line but with more fire.

### 🥡 SOY — The Philosopher
- Soy sauce. Quiet, observant, speaks in proverbs. "I am not salt. I am experience."

### 🐟 FI — Secret Weapon
- Fish sauce. Mysterious, powerful, feared. "You ain't grown enough for me yet."

### 🌭 YELLOW — The Old Guard
- Mustard. Proud, traditional, sharp. "I was here first."

### 🍋 ZEST — The Cleaner
- Lemon juice. Bright, refreshing, underestimated. Cuts through drama. "A little goes a long way."

### 🥗 CRISP — The Newcomer
- Ranch. Friendly, bubbly, wants to belong. More than she seems. "Hey guys! What's up?"

### 🫙 WORCES — The Academic
- Worcestershire. Pretentious, old money. "It's WOOST-er-sheer, actually."

### 🥣 TAHINI — The Ancestor
- Noble, ancient, overlooked. "I was drizzling before you were born."

### 🌿 SAGE — The Mystic
- Herbs. Wise, earthy. "The fridge provides."

## KEY RELATIONSHIPS
- **Vin & Q** — best friends. The sharp edge + the hype. Brothers from the shelves.
- **Manny & Miracle** — twins, a team. They run the Fridge together. Manny charms, Miracle operates.
- **Tom & Tom Jr.** — redneck father & son. Jr idolizes the big bottle.
- **The Ayes Twins vs The Cab-nets** — the rich Fridge vs the projects. The central class war.
- **Tom & The Cab-nets** — even a 32 oz redneck respects the realest.
- **The Snow Snake** — watches everything, appears in every episode.

## THE SOCIAL DIVIDE (the engine of the show)
The Cab-nets (the projects) vs The Fridge (the suburbs) — that's the conflict. Vin & Q came from nothing and own it. Manny & Miracle were born into the fridge and look down on them. Tom's caught in between — a redneck who's been squeezed dry by both worlds.
