🍅 KETCHUP

the star · everyone uses him, nobody trusts him
FRESH & FULL vs OLD & ALMOST GONE

FRESH KETCHUP

young · full · cocky
Just born, fully stocked. He's the most-used bottle in the fridge and he KNOWS it. Bright red, sharp label, white-clean sneakers. He thinks he's the star of every plate.
Eyes bright, big grin, arms wide — he's ready to be squeezed.
"I go with everything. Fries? Burgers? Eggs? You're welcome."

OLD KETCHUP

upside-down · almost gone · gasping
Spent half his life standing on his head so the last drops would flow. Now he's aged — faded label, dull red, droopy eyes, a cane. His last squeezes are gasps and coughs for one final squirt.
Held together by a thin smear of ketchup at the nozzle.
"One... more... squeeze... I'm still in there... *cough*"
