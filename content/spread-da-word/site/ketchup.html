KETCHUP JR.

POP (KETCHUP SR.)

the dad · more than half used · bitter
Half his life spent standing on his head so the last drops would come out. Now he's translucent — you can see right through him, just a smear left. Faded label, droopy grumpy eyes, grey stubble, arms crossed. He's bitter: he's been squeezed dry by everyone.
Face stays upright at the bottom even though his world is flipped.
"You kids think ketchup's a joke. Try being everyone's first squeeze for 25 years. *hmph*"
