🎨 BEHIND THE SCENES

How the condiment universe gets made — rigs, tools, and process

🎬 THE PROCESS

  • Roster → Rig → Scene → Episode. Every character starts as canon in the roster, gets a cutout rig, then appears in scenes.
  • Style: South Park-style cutout — flat shapes, simple joints, no gradients. Fast to produce, instantly readable.
  • Tool: Rive (free, browser-native) is the winning vote for the final animation. Rigs are built to import straight into Rive.
  • Music: 8-bit chiptune generated in code — square/triangle oscillators + noise. No samples, no licensing, fully original.
  • The Snow Snake 🐍 appears in backgrounds. Always watching. Never explained.

🧊 THE WORLD

  • The Hood — cabinet condiments who grew up unopened, dreaming of the fridge. Origin stories live here.
  • The Fridge — the ones who made it out. The politics play out here.
  • Wild Cards — recurring guests who leave an impression.
  • Relationships are the real hook — Vin & Que, Mayo & Whip, Red & Yellow, Crisp & Aji.
