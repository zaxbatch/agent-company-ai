FAQs

Our free trial is completely free, with no commitment to purchase. You don't need to enter your credit card details, so once your 7 days are over there are no automatic charges.

You can sign up for a 7 day free trial here.

All videos you create during your trial will contain the VideoScribe watermark unless you purchase a subscription.

If you are a freelancer, agency or business creating videos on behalf of a third party, i.e. reselling the videos you create, or charging for the use of your videos in any way, you must purchase a Max plan as these include reseller rights.

If you are a business or an individual user creating videos for commercial or personal use but not reselling your videos, then you can choose the plan most suited to your requirements and how much you intend to use VideoScribe. 

If you require team licences (i.e multiple licences under one subscription) you need to buy Core or Max subscriptions.

Commercial rights (included in all subscription plans) enable you to create videos that you can use to promote a business or product.

These differ from reseller rights (only included in Max plans), enabling you to resell the videos you create within VideoScribe to third parties. This is necessary if you are a freelancer or business that produces videos on behalf of a third party or client.

If you are creating videos within VideoScribe for a third-party and charging them for the creation of that video, or charging for the use of that video in any way, you require reseller rights. Reseller rights are only included in the Max plan.

AI tools — like image generation, scriptwriting, and voiceovers — use a lot of computing power behind the scenes. Every time you generate content, powerful servers are working hard to deliver those results in real time. That kind of performance requires significant resources, which can get pretty expensive.

To keep things running smoothly for everyone and ensure fair usage, we’ve set limits to balance access and performance. These limits help us maintain quality, keep costs down, and continue improving your creative experience with VideoScribe.

Yes, VideoScribe plans are recurring subscriptions. If you choose a monthly term, you will be charged every month on the same date. If you choose an annual term, you will be billed on the same date every year unless you cancel.
